# Code of Conduct

Be kind and respectful. This is a small project and everyone here is a volunteer.

- Be welcoming, patient and constructive. Assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Don't publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through [github.com/abhixdd](https://github.com/abhixdd).

Adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant.
